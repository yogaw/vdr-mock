01 · The Challenge

Confidential sharing is either uncontrolled or overpriced

Today, confidential documents travel through email threads, generic drive links, and manually controlled folders. Once a document leaves, control is lost: there is no view-only mode, no per-viewer traceability, and no way to revoke access in the middle of a transaction.

Conventional enterprise VDR platforms restore that control, but their commercial model works against you: a large one-time setup and add-on fees for storage, support, and retention that compound over the life of every deal.

  • No view-only mode: documents, once shared, can be forwarded and downloaded freely
  • No traceability of who viewed what, and when
  • No mid-deal revocation: access cannot be withdrawn once granted
  • Add-on creep: storage, support, and retention fees stack on top

05 · Platform

Production-grade, leanly built

Production discipline without overengineered infrastructure, sized for the transaction and ready to grow.

Google Cloud architecture

Cloud Run, Cloud SQL (PostgreSQL), Cloud Storage, Identity Platform, Secret Manager, and Cloud Logging, with managed HTTPS throughout.

Documents never public

Private object storage, short-lived signed access, and server-side authorization evaluated on every request. Originals are exposed only when download is explicitly granted.

## 1. The Challenge

Confidential document sharing today relies on email threads, generic drive links, and
manually controlled folders. Once a document leaves, control is lost: no view-only mode, no
per-viewer traceability, no way to revoke access mid-transaction. Conventional enterprise
VDR platforms restore control — but at the cost of setup fees, minimum personnel licenses,
and per-seat monthly billing that compounds over the life of a deal.

## 2. What We Deliver

A purpose-built VDR, hosted on Google Cloud, engineered around four outcomes:

- **Secure viewing** — documents open in a controlled in-browser viewer; originals stay in
  private storage and are never exposed through public URLs.
- **Dynamic watermarking** — every view is stamped with the viewer's identity, the project,
  and a live timestamp: deterrence, traceability, accountability.
- **Download control** — view-only by default; download enabled per room or per document,
  enforced server-side on every request.
- **Complete audit trail** — logins, document opens, downloads, uploads, permission changes,
  and access revocations, recorded with user, timestamp, and IP.

Plus: access expiration, immediate revocation, bulk upload, version replacement,
folder-based organization, and large-file-friendly progressive viewing.

## 6. Platform at a Glance

- **Google Cloud architecture** — Cloud Run, Cloud SQL (PostgreSQL), Cloud Storage,

- **Documents never public** — private storage, short-lived signed access, server-side
